Maine jo kuch bhi socha hua hai main woh waqt aane pe kar jaunga
Tum mujhe zeher lagte ho aur main kisi din tumhein pee ke mar jaunga

Raaten kisi yaad mein katti hain aur din daftar kha jata hai
Dil jeene pe mail hota hai to maut ka dar kha jata hai

> Is shayari mein shayar ne dil ke dukh aur office/daftar ki thaka dene wali zindagi ke beech ke takraav ko behad khubsoorat andaaz mein pesh kiya hai.
