_Kis qadar seedha sahal saaf hai rasta dekho_

_Na kisi shakh ka saya hai, na deewar ki oat_

**Tum** agar saath ho to safar bhi dua lagta hai,
warna har mod pe insaan ko apna hi sawal milta hai.

> Kuch alfaaz thahar kar padho. Shayri ka asar aksar beech ki khamoshi mein hota hai.
