Baad mein mujhse na kehna ghar palatna theek hai
Waise sunne mein yahi aaya hai raasta theek hai

Shaakh se patta gire, baarish ruke, baadal chhate
Main hi to sab kuch galat karta hoon, accha theek hai

> Har cheez ke kharab hone par khud par ilzam lene ke tanz ko shayar ne bade masoomana andaz mein pesh kiya hai.
