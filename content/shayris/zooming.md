Dekhun jo aasman se to itni baDi zamin  
Itni baDi zamin pe chhoTa sa ek shahr  
ChhoTe se ek shahr mein saDkon ka ek jal  
SaDkon ke jal mein chhupi viran si gali  
Viran gali ke moD pe tanha sa ek shajar  
Tanha shajar ke sae mein chhoTa sa ek makan  
ChhoTe se ek makan mein kachchi zamin ka sahn  
Kachchi zamin ke sahn mein khilta hua gulab  
Khilte hue gulab mein mahka hua badan  
Mahke hue badan mein samundar sa ek dil  
Us dil ki wusaton mein kahin kho gaya hun main  

Yun hai ki is zamin se baDa ho gaya hun main
